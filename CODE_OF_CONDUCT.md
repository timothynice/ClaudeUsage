# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, personal
appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Respectful communication and constructive feedback
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them become contributors
- Being open to different viewpoints

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Sharing private information without consent
- Any conduct that creates a hostile environment

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers at:
https://github.com/<OWNER>/<REPO>/issues

The maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, or other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
